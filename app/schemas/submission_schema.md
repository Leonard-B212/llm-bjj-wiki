# Example (Darce-Choke)
---

## Setup

* From Turtle or Front Headlock

## Durchführung

### Entry

* Thread arm deep under opponent’s armpit and past the head
* Reach through fully (do not stop at the neck)
* Lock hands using a gable/baseball grip

### Finish

* Drive opponent onto their side or forward
* Apply shoulder pressure into the neck
* Keep chest tight (no space)
* Drive hips in and increase pressure gradually

## Probleme

* Arm not deep enough → no pressure
* Too much space between chest and opponent
* Opponent rolls → lose position
* Wrong angle → choke not effective
* Trying to finish too early

## Hints

* Control position before finishing
* Pressure comes from chest + shoulder, not arms
* Angle is more important than strength

## Wenn blockiert / In Kombination mit

* [[Anaconda Choke]]
* [[Front-Headlock]]
* [[Guillotine-Choke]]

## Verteidigung

* Keep arms tight (no underhook allowed)
* Hide the head
* Roll early before the lock is secured
